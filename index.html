NUMBER UP

A design laboratory. You are a dot, and the dot is the current Number. Moving is a superposition event: your Number and the destination tile interact, and a new Number emerges. What that interaction means is exactly what each variant below proposes differently. Pick one and feel it.

Worlds are switchable while playing (N cycles hand-authored maps, G generates a seeded one). Rules can also be cycled live with C and F.
